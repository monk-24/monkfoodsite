About us

Your technology partner — not just another vendor

Most hospitality businesses get sold a system and left to cope. Monk Food works as your consultant: we learn how you trade, build the right roadmap, and stay with you long after the sale.

Our story

Built for owners, not middlemen

For too long, hospitality businesses have been sold systems they don't need — the wrong tech, mismatched to how they actually trade, with support that vanishes the moment the invoice is paid. Monk Food was built to end that.

We work as consultants first. We take the time to understand how your business runs, then draw a clear roadmap across the three things that actually move the needle: the right system support, a strong digital presence, and the best payment solution for your venue. No off-the-shelf guesswork, no paying for features you'll never use.

And unlike the old model — juggling different providers who never pick up the phone once the sale is done — Monk Food is one partner for all of it. We're with you from onboarding and we stay for the long term, right through to the day you're ready for your next upgrade. When others disappear after the sale, we're still here.
